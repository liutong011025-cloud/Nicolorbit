# Nicolorbit

**Want to help Nicole, too?**

Welcome to Nicolorbit—a growing collection of thoughtful tools and workflows, made to make good work a little easier.

## Skills

| Skill | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `nicolorbit-find-literature` | Finds authentic, relevant education technology research and adds APA 7 in-text citations and reference entries. |
| `nicolorbit-check-citations` | Cross-checks manuscripts and reference lists, repairs verifiable omissions, and highlights unresolved mismatches. |
| `nicolorbit-create-ppt` | Extends existing presentations with concise, visual, fully sourced content while learning the deck's style. |
| `nicolorbit-translate-ppt` | Translates PowerPoint decks into polished English while preserving layout, typography, emphasis, and embedded media. |

## Design principles

- Evidence comes before citation: sources must be real, traceable, and relevant to the claim.
- Documents stay editable and retain their existing structure and visual language.
- Presentation content stays concise, visual, and readable.
- Uncertainty is marked clearly instead of being filled with invented details.
- Outputs are checked for citation consistency, layout problems, and source integrity before delivery.

## Example prompts

```text
Use $nicolorbit-find-literature to add verified APA 7 support to the claims in this manuscript.

Use $nicolorbit-check-citations to reconcile this article with its reference list and highlight unresolved mismatches.

Use $nicolorbit-create-ppt to add these materials to my deck while matching its style and citing every source.

Use $nicolorbit-translate-ppt to translate this presentation into English without changing its visual identity.
```

Nicolorbit is designed for careful research and communication workflows. It does not treat search snippets as evidence, invent missing references, or flatten editable presentations into screenshots.
