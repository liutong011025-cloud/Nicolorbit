# Nicolorbit Find Literature

Edit the supplied manuscript while preserving its wording, structure, formatting, and existing citations. Produce a new document containing the original text with added citations and a complete APA 7 reference list.

## Identify claims that need evidence

- Treat empty parentheses, citation placeholders, and explicit user markers as required research locations.
- Also identify factual, causal, comparative, prevalence, effectiveness, and theoretical claims that would normally require scholarly support even when no placeholder is present.
- Do not add citations to personal reflections, transitions, or claims already adequately supported unless another source materially improves accuracy.
- One source may support several nearby claims, but every inserted citation must genuinely entail the sentence it follows.

## Find and verify sources

- Search for peer-reviewed, directly relevant research in leading education technology and learning-science journals. Prefer journals such as *Computers & Education*, *British Journal of Educational Technology*, *Educational Technology Research and Development*, *The Internet and Higher Education*, *Journal of Computer Assisted Learning*, *Learning, Media and Technology*, and *International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education*. Use other strong journals when they are a better substantive fit.
- Prefer systematic reviews, meta-analyses, major empirical studies, and foundational theory papers. Balance recency with authority; do not exclude a seminal older source merely because it is old.
- Verify each work through a primary or authoritative record: publisher page, DOI landing page, Crossref metadata, or the journal's official index. Confirm title, authors, year, journal, volume, issue, pages or article number, and DOI.
- Never invent a source, DOI, author, date, quotation, finding, or journal status. Do not cite a paper based only on a search-result snippet or an unverified secondary citation.
- Read enough of the abstract or full text to confirm that the study supports the precise claim and population. Avoid stretching adjacent findings into stronger claims.
- If no defensible source exists, leave the prose unchanged and flag the unsupported claim in the delivery note instead of adding a weak citation.

## Insert APA 7 citations

- Use parenthetical citations such as `(Author, 2024)` and narrative citations such as `Author (2024)` according to the sentence grammar.
- Apply APA 7 author rules: one author; two authors joined by `&` in parentheses; three or more authors shortened to first author plus `et al.` from the first citation.
- Order multiple works in one parenthesis alphabetically and separate them with semicolons.
- Add page or paragraph locators for direct quotations. Do not present paraphrases as quotations.
- Place citations immediately after the supported proposition, before terminal punctuation where APA requires it. Remove an empty placeholder after replacing it.
- Preserve the manuscript's language. Do not rewrite claims unless the user requested editing or a small grammatical change is essential to integrate a narrative citation.

## Build the reference list

- If the document already has a reference list, merge new entries into it, remove exact duplicates, and retain valid existing entries.
- Format new entries in APA 7, alphabetize by first-author surname, and use hanging indents in editable documents.
- Include DOI links in `https://doi.org/...` form when available. Do not add database names or retrieval dates unless APA 7 calls for them.
- Cross-check every new in-text citation against exactly one new or existing reference entry before delivery.

## Deliver and verify

- For a DOCX, edit a copy and preserve styles, tables, comments, headers, footers, and pagination as closely as practical. Render the finished document and inspect every affected page.
- For pasted text, return the full original text with inserted citations followed by a clearly labeled APA 7 reference list.
- Provide a compact source audit listing the claim location, inserted citation, and authoritative verification link. This audit is supplementary and must not replace the edited manuscript.
- State any claim for which no reliable supporting source could be verified.
